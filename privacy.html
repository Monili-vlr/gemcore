Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026. This policy covers the website monili-vlr.github.io/gemcore. It follows the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679).

1. Controller and contact

The controller is Valerio Scorretti, founder of Monili VLR and owner of the GemCore project. Contact for any privacy request: the contact form.

2. What this website does not do

No cookies. No analytics or tracking. No advertising. No forms except the contact form, which sends your message only when you press Send. No fonts, scripts or images loaded from other websites: everything is served from this site. The video plays only when you press play and is loaded from this site.

4. Recipients and transfers outside the EU

  • GitHub, Inc. (USA) hosts this website on GitHub Pages and processes the technical data above. See GitHub’s privacy statement on github.com.
  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) delivers the messages of the contact form, and Google provides the mailbox where they arrive (Gmail); both may process them in the United States.

Both providers may process data in the United States. Transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, and on the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR). We do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

5. How long we keep data

  • Hosting logs: kept by GitHub under its own policy; we have no access to them.
  • Emails: as long as needed to answer and follow up your request, and no longer than 24 months after the last contact, unless a contract or the law requires longer.

6. Your rights (Articles 15-21 GDPR)

You can ask to access your data (Art. 15), correct it (Art. 16), delete it (Art. 17), restrict its processing (Art. 18), receive it in a portable format (Art. 20) and object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21). We also tell recipients about corrections, deletions or restrictions (Art. 19). Write to us through the contact form; we answer within one month.

You can also complain to a data protection authority, in particular in the EU country where you live or work. In Italy: Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, garanteprivacy.it.

7. No automated decisions

We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing, including profiling, that produce legal or similarly significant effects on you (Art. 22 GDPR).

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
