Free modelling

Have a photo? We model it for free.

Send us 1 to 8 photos of the ring you want, from different sides. We model it with GemCore at no cost; you pay only if you order the wax print or the finished jewel. A perfect copy of the shape is not guaranteed.

What to send

  • Photos (JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB each).
  • What kind of ring it is (solitaire, band, signet…).
  • Size, metal and stones, if you know them.

How

For now by email. Your photos are used only to model your ring.
